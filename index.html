How it works

From discovery to contact in three steps

01 — AI Optimization

AI Match Optimization & Alignment

Input your venture profile or fund thesis. Our AI neural matching engine evaluates traction, stage readiness, and strategic alignment to curate high-conviction matches — moving beyond manual keyword filters.

02 — Compare

Compare up to 3 listings side by side

Add any listings to your compare tray. See funding amounts, eligibility, terms, and provider details in a clear, structured view. Save the ones that fit.

03 — Connect

Contact providers directly

Reach out to the provider directly through their listing. Finance Connect stays out of the decision — no middleman, no fees, no applications processed on your behalf.
